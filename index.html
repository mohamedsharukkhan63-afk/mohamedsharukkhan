01 · OUR HOME

A small coastal village with a big story.

Valinokkam sits on a peninsula along the Gulf of Mannar, where everyday life is closely connected to the sea.

Its coastline, fishing activity, salt production, backwater landscapes and community life make it a distinctive part of Ramanathapuram district.
